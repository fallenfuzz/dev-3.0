Short: Pinned Claude logins shown everywhere

When a project's env sets `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, its login now appears as "Project login (…/proj/.claude)" with that account's email: on the launch dialogs' account pill (including agent-requested launches), as its own row with its own 5h/7d usage in the header usage panel, and in Settings -> Accounts with the projects that pin it. Pinned sessions no longer feed their usage into the default login's row, which is now labelled "Default login (<full path>)" instead of "System login (~/.claude)" and is left out of the usage panel when it has no account, no usage, and no managed account to choose between. The pill no longer claims the pin while a dev3-managed Claude account overrides it. Beside project pins, the default login is also left out when nobody is signed into it, so usage older builds filed under it no longer shows as a phantom login; Settings marks it "Not signed in", explains that pinned projects always use their own login, and drops the radio circle from pinned rows.
